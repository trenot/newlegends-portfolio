# How to Add Your Real Concert Photography to NewLegends

You have **two very easy ways** to replace the placeholder photos with your actual live concert photography:

---

## Method 1: In-Browser Quick Replacer (Zero Code / Drag & Drop)

1. Open the website (`index.html`) in your browser.
2. Click the **"Photo Manager"** button in the header, or click **"Replace / Upload Photo"** on any individual concert page.
3. You can either:
   - **Upload directly from your computer**: Click "Choose Image" to select a photo from your camera roll or desktop. It will automatically be compressed to high-res web quality and saved directly to your browser's local storage.
   - **Or enter a local path / image URL**: Type in a filename like `assets/photos/5sos-msg-1.jpg` or a web link from your portfolio / Wix / Dropbox / Google Drive.
4. Click **"Save Photo"** — the page updates instantly!

---

### Recommended Image Specs:
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:2 landscape or 16:9 for hero banners; 4:5 or 1:1 works great for the Photo Vault.
- **Resolution**: 1600px - 2500px wide (JPG or WebP recommended).
- **Color Profile**: sRGB.
